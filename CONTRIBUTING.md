# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome. Before you start, read:

- [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md#rule-1-never-look-at-calibres-source-code): the
  project is written without calibre's code, and contributions must be too.
- The [requirements](docs/requirements/README.md) and the
  [GitHub issues](https://github.com/endqwerty/ebook-manager/issues) for what
  the app should do next.

## Protected books

Ebook Manager keeps books locked by a protection scheme in the library and
says why it cannot open them. The project does not ship, link to, host or
describe a way to remove protection from a book. Issues, pull requests,
comments and documentation that ask for one, offer one or point to one are
closed or removed.
