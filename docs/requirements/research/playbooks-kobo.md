## Part 1. Google Play Books

### Uploads
- Accepted types: PDF and EPUB only. Verified: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/11012086
- PDFs need "Enable PDF uploading" in Play Books settings; EPUBs do not. Upload routes: Android "Open with / Upload to Play Books" from Files or Downloads, and the Gmail attachment action. Uploads live under Library > Uploads. Verified, same page.
- Bookmarks, highlights and notes sync for PDFs only if uploaded after 20 January 2026; older uploaded PDFs are excluded. Verified, same page. (A new-feature cutoff stored per file.)
- Limits: 1,000 files and 50 MB per file at the 2013 launch; later reports say 100 MB per file. [unverified] for today: no current Google page stated a limit. Sources: https://shellypalmer.com/2013/05/google-play-books-upload/ , https://www.xatakandroid.com/aplicaciones-android/google-play-books-ahora-permite-subir-libros-de-hasta-100-mb-por-archivo-el-doble-que-antes (search summaries only).
- DRM-protected uploads stall at "processing" forever. [unverified, old review via search summary]
- Upload processing: one guide says Google converts every uploaded EPUB to its own internal format and that HTML5 tags, scripts or embedded media, or missing metadata, can break it. [unverified, secondary: https://ostechnix.com/fix-epub-processing-error-google-play-books/ ; https://commonsware.com/blog/2017/03/01/google-play-books-epubs.html]
- Shelves for uploads and series for uploads: the shelves page does not mention uploads. Verified only that shelves and series exist for the library. Whether uploads join Series is [unverified]. A J-Novel Club forum post reports later purchased volumes missing from the series; Google support said it could not see that data. [unverified, https://forums.j-novel.club/post/85052]

### Library organisation (verified, https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9402600)
- Shelves: create, name, long-press to add, remove from shelf. Bulk actions: add to shelf, download, remove download, mark finished or not finished, add to or remove from Family Library, hide.
- Hide moves items to a Hidden library (not shown on other devices); permanent delete removes from all devices and Family Library, cannot be undone, and ends refund eligibility for purchases. Hidden series can be restored with all books or card-only.
- Series view lists books you do not own too; filter Owned/Unowned; sort Series order or Reverse. "View series" from a book.
- Filters: genre, author, progress (Not started), format, subscription status. Alphabetical index only appears above 25 books and not when sorted by recent.

### Reader settings (verified, https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9755756)
- Text tab: font size (pinch), typeface, line spacing, alignment (Default/Left/Justify), page layout (Auto, 1 page, 2 page). "Remember zoom" for Original pages view.
- Lighting tab: brightness, Light/Sepia/Dark, Reading Night Light (also follows the phone's Night Light).
- Font style, alignment and layout options exist only in some books; display colour in most. Original pages versus flowing text is a per-book choice; Read Aloud needs flowing text.
- Tapping the page shows reading stats; slider and page thumbnail return to start.
- Page numbers differ from the store listing because text reflows. Verified: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/3238095
- Device limit: most books download to about 3 to 5 devices at once; most textbooks one. Verified, same page.

### Notes, highlights, Drive (verified, https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/3165868)
- Settings toggle saves notes, highlights and bookmarks to Drive, one document per book, in a folder "Play Books Notes" (renameable).
- Notes made before the toggle only sync after a new note triggers a document containing all notes for that book.
- Highlighted text can be missing if the publisher disabled copy-paste or the count exceeds the publisher's copy-paste maximum; page number link still lets you check.
- Edits in the Drive document are overwritten when new notes are added; one-way sync, copy first.
- Other: Define (one word), Translate, Search from a selection; notes list under Contents.

### Read aloud (verified, https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/11938821)
- Mobile apps only, flowing text, publisher decides per book. "Natural voice" needs internet; offline needs it switched off. Voice, language and speed come from the device text-to-speech settings; the default TTS engine is used. Works with TalkBack. No sleep timer or language list on the page.
- Auto-narrated audiobooks use a digital voice and users report mispronunciations and odd pauses. [unverified, search summary]

### Kids and reading practice (verified, https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/10187766)
- Tap a word to hear it; dictionary lookup readable aloud; Read & listen (narrated children's books, can auto-turn pages); Practice reading (English narrated children's books only, follows the child and helps with words); bookmarks.

### Audiobooks
- Speed (1x default, up to 2x), sleep timer (set time or end of chapter), 30-second skip, chapter titles, standard or high quality download, Assistant voice control, position sync. All from an archived 2021 help page and reviews, so [unverified] for today: https://webcf.waybackmachine.org/web/20211006101154/https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7572879 , https://play.google.com/about/books/ . "Smart resume" was not found anywhere.

### 2024 to 2026 additions
- Book insights (Gemini): "Catch me up" summaries and Q&A that use only text up to the reader's current position (spoiler-safe); over 1 million titles carry a Tools badge; mainly English; iOS rollout began 2 September 2026 after web and Android. Verified from a secondary article: https://jetstream.blog/en/ios-book-insights-rollout/
- Expert Intelligence (27 August 2026): purchased books (over 100,000 titles from named publishers) can be added as sources to Gemini Notebook; the reader must own the book; shared notebooks need each person to own it; uploads not mentioned. Verified: https://9to5google.com/2026/08/27/gemini-notebook-play-books/
- PDF annotation sync (20 January 2026) above.
- Adobe Digital Editions: from 1 June 2026 it signs in with a ByteBooks ID, not Adobe ID; export service unavailable for earlier-downloaded EPUBs. Verified: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/179863

### Export
- Per-book Export on the web library: Download EPUB or PDF, or an ACSM that needs ADE. Not available for rentals, some publishers, large files. Each purchase is bound to one Adobe ID; most Google books will not open on Kindle. Verified, https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/179863. The page does not cover exporting uploads.
- Google Takeout contents for Play Books: one 2018 forum reply says uploaded books can be re-downloaded from takeout.google.com. [unverified, old]

### Repeated complaints (mostly [unverified], from old forum posts and a commentary)
- Uploaded EPUB covers missing or wrong; processing errors; wrong volume delivered by ACSM files for purchased series (MobileRead p=4396110). Cannot import by scanning a folder; titles added one at a time. Annotation sync gaps for uploaded PDFs before Jan 2026.

## Part 2. Rakuten Kobo

### Formats (verified, https://help.kobo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017763713-File-formats-your-Kobo-eReader-and-Kobo-Books-app-support)
- eReaders and desktop app: EPUB, EPUB2, EPUB3, PDF, MOBI (page also lists "FlePub", a typo), TXT, HTML, RTF, JPEG, GIF, PNG, BMP, TIFF, CBZ, CBR. Original Kobo and Kobo Wi-Fi lack EPUB3.
- iOS app: EPUB, PDF, MOBI. Android app: EPUB, MOBI, no PDF listed. Books with audio/video (ePub3AV) are listed for apps only.
- ACSM files are Adobe DRM; ADE works only with eReaders, not apps. Social DRM opens without ADE.

### Kepub vs plain EPUB (MobileRead wiki, undated and old: https://wiki.mobileread.com/wiki/Kepub)
- Kepub wraps sentences in uniquely identified spans that Kobo's position system uses. Adds highlights, multiple bookmarks, dictionary lookup, annotations, faster page turns, footnote previews, double-tap image zoom, image dithering, minutes left in chapter, automatic fallback fonts for missing glyphs.
- Plain EPUB has better hyphenation, full justification, embedded fonts, word spacing (attributed to kepub reader bugs). Renaming to .kepub.epub does not give a true kepub: reopening loses position and annotations. Whether features apply to sideloads has changed over firmware; treat as [unverified] for current firmware.
- Kobo's own list omits kepub entirely.

### Collections, series, sideloads
- Kobo renamed Shelves to Collections; collections can hold sideloaded books and be created manually (2016 forum). Nothing about sideloaded books, including their collections, syncs to Kobo servers; collection names do. [unverified, MobileRead forum summaries]
- Sideloaded books do not appear in the Kobo apps; Android app needs "import items". [unverified]

### Stats, Reading Life, awards
- Stats: total reading time, average session length, pages per minute, time to finish, percent of library read; awards for completing goals. Official-era text: all models, but only for Kobo-store EPUBs. Sources: https://blog.the-ebook-reader.com/2021/07/10/kobo-ereaders-software-features-list/ , https://goodereader.com/blog/electronic-readers/do-reading-statistics-matter-on-e-readers (undated). Forum reports: awards and pages-per-minute do not update for unsynced sideloaded kepubs; sideloads in Finished state count toward books read. [unverified]
- StoryGraph integration (live June 2026, free): syncs only "Currently Reading" progress as a percentage, automatically; no highlights, notes or back-sync; disconnecting keeps shared data; store, Kobo Plus and Libby loans work, sideloaded books do not. Verified: https://help.kobo.com/hc/en-us/articles/40276991427223-Sync-Your-Kobo-Reading-Progress-with-StoryGraph ; sideload exclusion from search summaries.

### Annotations and export
- Text highlights: "Export Annotations" on a long-press, written as text files into an "Exported Annotations" folder; enabling needs `ExportHighlights=true` in the hidden `.kobo/Kobo/eReader.conf`; no automatic updates; works on Libra Colour, Libra 2, Clara Colour per one author; truncation reports exist. Readwise import covers only Kobo-store books. Verified (single blog): https://greg-morris.micro.blog/2024/05/08/exporting-highlights-from.html
- A newer official export to PDF, HTML, TXT, Markdown for purchased and Kobo Plus books is reported but not confirmed on a Kobo page. [unverified]
- Stylus markup on Kobo ePub/kepub/PDF on Sage, Elipsa, Elipsa 2E, Libra Colour (Stylus or Stylus 2): saved to the device only; not on apps or other eReaders; lost on reset or sign-out; may show in brackets after a font size change; only non-DRM PDFs export with handwriting (via USB; EPUB markup cannot export). Verified: https://help.kobo.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500001927562-Write-notes-in-your-book-with-Kobo-Stylus
- Notebooks (verified via French page: https://help.kobo.com/hc/fr/articles/360062226733): Basic (free drawing, backgrounds, lasso, shape snap; export PNG, JPEG, PDF, ZIP; starts with 2 pages) and Advanced (write between lines, handwriting to text, math, diagrams; export DOCX, text, HTML, ZIP; no page limit). Export whole notebook or one page to linked Dropbox or USB. Notebooks sync to kobo.com by default (view-only there); unsynced notebooks are erased on sign-out or reset. Avoid special characters in names for Dropbox.

## Data the app must store
- Per file: origin (store, upload, sideload), DRM type and the account or ID binding (Adobe ID or ByteBooks ID), "processing" state, import date, and a flag whether annotations sync for it (Google PDF cutoff 2026-01-20).
- Reading position in two forms: reflowable locator and fixed-page "original pages" position, plus per-book view mode and remembered zoom.
- Per-book layout overrides (font, spacing, alignment, columns Auto/1/2, theme, night light) and whether the book allows each.
- Annotations with: type (highlight, note, bookmark, ink stroke), colour, text snippet, page label, locator, whether text was truncated by a copy limit, modified time, and export state (last exported, destination file id, edited-externally conflict).
- Ink: stylus strokes anchored to a locator or page, with a "may be displaced after reflow" state; notebooks with kind (basic, advanced), pages, recognised text, backgrounds, sync flag.
- Collections: manual and automatic (series-derived), hidden state, "not owned" series members, owned/unowned filter, per-device collection membership.
- Device records: download count per book against a licence limit, last-sync time, per-device fonts folder, supported formats and EPUB3 ability, firmware version.
- Reading sessions (time, pages per minute), goals, awards, "finished" flag, StoryGraph-style external link status and last pushed percentage.
- Saved articles: source URL, service (Instapaper), read state.
- Library loans: library card id, due date, ISBN-13 plus publication date used for matching.
- Fonts: family, file name, weight and sharpness overrides, embedded-vs-user override rule.

## Requirements to adopt that existing products get wrong
1. Treat sideloaded and uploaded files as full citizens: stats, awards, series, annotations and sync identical to store books.
2. Give every annotation an export path in open formats (Markdown, JSON, plain text) without a hidden config flag, and two-way safe: never overwrite external edits.
3. Never lose ink on reflow; anchor to text and keep an original snapshot.
4. Document upload limits, count, size and type, in the app, and show a clear failure reason instead of an endless "processing".
5. Record why a highlight text is missing (copy limits) and offer a fallback.
6. Back up notebooks and handwriting off-device by default, with an export that never erases on sign-out.
7. Provide a repair tool for sync state, and a per-book view of exactly what is synced.
8. Let series appear for any book, including those a user does not own.
9. Allow multiple library cards and audiobook loans.
10. Offer a read-aloud engine setting with per-book availability and offline voices, plus a sleep timer.
