# Research notes

The reports the [requirements](../README.md) are drawn from, as the research agents
wrote them in November 2026. They are kept as evidence: each lists the pages it
opened and marks every claim as read on a page, taken from a search summary, or
unverified. They are working notes and have not been edited for style. Where a note
and a requirements page disagree, the requirements page is the current view.

A first round of nine shorter reports covered the same subjects at feature-list
level. Their content is folded into the requirements pages and they are not kept.

Every agent worked under
[Rule 1](../../../AGENTS.md#rule-1-never-look-at-calibres-source-code). Where a note
mentions calibre, it describes behaviour as users report it, or names a page the agent
declined to open. One agent requested a made-up address on the website of calibre's
author by mistake. The request returned "not found" and nothing was read.
